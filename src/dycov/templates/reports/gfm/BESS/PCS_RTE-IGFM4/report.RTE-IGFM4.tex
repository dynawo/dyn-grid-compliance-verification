    \section{Results for PCS RTE-IGFM4}

    Test 4 of the DTR Fiche I18 (Grid Forming), for non-synchronous storage units of type C
    and D: the frequency of the infinite bus ramps from 49.5~Hz at
    +0.5~Hz/s during 3~s and at +2~Hz/s during 500~ms, and from 50.5~Hz at the opposite rates,
    with a grid of short-circuit ratio 2, 10 and 20, from the operating points OC1 to OC5. The
    envelopes bound the active current \(I_p\) at the PGU terminals.

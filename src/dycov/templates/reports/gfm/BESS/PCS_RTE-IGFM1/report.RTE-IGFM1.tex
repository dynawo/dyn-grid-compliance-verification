    \section{Results for PCS RTE-IGFM1}

    Test 1 of the DTR Fiche I18 (Grid Forming), for non-synchronous storage units of type C
    and D: the phase of the voltage at the infinite bus jumps by
    \(\Delta\theta_{grid} = \pm 0.3\,(x_{eff} + x_{grid})\) and \(\pm 0.6\,(x_{eff} + x_{grid})\)
    rad, with a grid of short-circuit ratio 2, 10 and 20, from the operating points OC1 to OC5.
    The envelopes bound the active current \(I_p\) at the PGU terminals.

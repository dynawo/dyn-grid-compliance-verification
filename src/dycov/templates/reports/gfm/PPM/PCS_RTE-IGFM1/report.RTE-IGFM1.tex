    \section{Results for PCS RTE-IGFM1}

    Grid Forming test agreed with RTE before the DTR Fiche I18, which covers storage units
    alone: the phase of the voltage at the infinite bus jumps by
    \(\Delta\theta_{grid} = \pm 0.3\,(x_{eff} + x_{grid})\) rad, with a grid of short-circuit
    ratio 2 and 10, from the operating points OC1 to OC5. The envelopes bound the active current
    \(I_p\) at the PGU terminals.
